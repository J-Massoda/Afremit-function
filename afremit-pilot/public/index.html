A more accountable journey

From a request to real clarity.

Follow an education fee through the pilot. The same careful handoffs inform the healthcare and construction journeys.

01 / 04

The school starts small.

Its finance team creates one fee request with an amount and a private student reference. No entire course catalogue is needed.

02 / 04

The payer sees the details.

A family member abroad checks the institution, purpose, amount, and reference before starting a test payment.

03 / 04

Every handoff is visible.

Afremit records a simulated held state and flags anything that needs review. Nothing is described as settled until the institution confirms it.

04 / 04

Both sides know the outcome.

The school confirms allocation to the request. The payer sees the update and an audit trail of the test journey.

Open for guided testing

A working pilot.
Clearly at pilot stage.

Afremit is inviting private institutions and service providers to test the steps around a payment request. Applications and pilot activity can involve real participants. Payments, balances, quotes and receipts in this version are simulated.

There are currently no live transfers, active payment or escrow provider, or insurance cover in this pilot. A production payments model depends on suitable partners and agreements.
