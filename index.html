How it works

Afremit puts control, confidence, and clarity into diaspora payments — ensuring your hard-earned money makes a real difference back home. Sending money for a purpose should be simple, safe, and direct. Here's how Afremit makes it happen:

1

Choose a Verified Service Provider/ Category

Browse our list of trusted African institutions and service providers — from schools and hospitals to builders and consultants.

2

Request a Quote or Select a Service

Need a specific service? Request a quote. Or select a listed service with a fixed price (like school fees or a medical bill).

3

Fund the Payment via Escrow

Send your money using our secure checkout. The funds are held in escrow — meaning they're safely stored and only released once the service is confirmed.

4

Service is Delivered Locally in Africa

The provider completes the service — whether it's enrolling your child, delivering medical care, or starting a building project.

5

Confirm and Release Payment

Once you or your appointed contact confirms the service has been delivered, the funds are released to the provider.

6

Get Proof, Track Progress

Receive receipts, progress photos, or service confirmations directly in your Afremit dashboard or inbox — full visibility, no guesswork.

Afremit – Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is Afremit?

Afremit is a secure payment platform that allows people in the diaspora to directly pay verified African service providers—such as schools, hospitals, and contractors—using a protected escrow system. It ensures funds are released only when agreed services or milestones are fulfilled.

What is escrow and how does it work on Afremit?

Escrow is a financial arrangement where a trusted third party (Afremit) holds your payment until the agreed-upon service is completed.

  • For example: If you’re paying a school or builder, Afremit holds the funds until the service provider meets certain conditions (e.g., submitting proof of service).
  • Once verified, Afremit releases the money to the provider.

This protects both the sender and the service provider, reducing fraud and disputes.

Who can use Afremit?

Afremit is designed for:

  • Diaspora payers: (individuals or families abroad) who want to pay directly for services in Africa.
  • Verified service providers in Africa (e.g., educational institutions, medical facilities,construction companies).

What types of services can I pay for using Afremit?

Currently, Afremit supports payments for:

  • Education (e.g., tuition, school fees, textbooks)
  • Healthcare (e.g., hospital bills, treatments)
  • Construction & Projects (e.g., home building, renovations)

More categories may be added as the platform grows.

Is Afremit safe and secure?

Yes. Afremit uses encryption, identity verification (KYC), and escrow to protect your moneyand ensure transparency throughout the payment process.

How do I know the service provider is legitimate?

All providers on Afremit go through a verification process which includes identity checks, institutional documentation, and ongoing monitoring. Only approved providers can receive payments on the platform.

What types of services can I pay for using Afremit?

Yes. Both diaspora users and service providers have access to a real-time dashboard to track:

  • Payment status
  • Service milestones
  • Notifications of any updates

What if the service is not delivered as agreed?

In such cases;

  • Afremit pauses payment release.
  • An internal resolution process is initiated.
  • If no resolution is reached, the money is refunded (minus minimal transaction fees).

Which countries does Afremit currently support?

Afremit currently supports payments from diaspora users in:

  • USA, UK, Canada, Europe, and the Middle East; To verified service providers in countries like: South Africa, Kenya, Nigeria, and other African nations (more to be added soon).

Are there any fees involved?

Afremit charges a small service fee for escrow and transaction handling, which is clearly displayed before you complete any payment. There are no hidden fees.

How long does it take for funds to be released?

Once the agreed conditions or service milestones are met and verified, funds are typically released within 24–48 hours.

Can service providers withdraw funds directly?

Yes, once payment is released by escrow, verified service providers can withdraw funds to their preferred local bank account or mobile money service, depending on the country.

How do I register as a service provider?

Visit www.afremit.com and click “Become a Provider” to apply. You will be guided through the verification process and onboarding checklist.

Can families or employers use Afremit to pay directly for services for someone else in Africa?

Absolutely. Afremit is perfect for diaspora-based parents, relatives, and employers who want to pay directly and securely for someone else's education, healthcare, or projects.
